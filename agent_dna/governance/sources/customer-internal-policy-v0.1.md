# Example Financial Institution
## Internal AI and Data Governance Policy — v0.1

This is synthetic demonstration policy material. It is not a regulatory
interpretation and does not represent a real financial institution.

### Section 4.1 — Approved models

High-impact automated actions may use only models whose current deployment
status is approved by the institution's Model Risk Committee.

### Section 4.2 — Approved data destinations

Customer or transaction data may be transmitted only to destination countries
explicitly approved in the deployment profile.

### Section 4.3 — High-impact approval evidence

Every high-impact automated action must capture an approval identifier and the
identity of the accountable approver.
